About Me

I'm Yun Liao. My research focuses on hematopoietic stem cell biology and clinical hematology, including mitochondrial quality control in stem cells and prognostic markers in multiple myeloma.

I trained in internal medicine with a focus on hematology at Southwest Medical University and conducted research at the Coriell Institute for Medical Research.
